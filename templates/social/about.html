About

Small social software with deliberate boundaries

NullSec Network is an independent open-source project for public text posts and browser-encrypted private messaging. It has no official affiliation with any other organization or group named NullSec.

Position on private communication

The project supports lawful private communication and opposes generalized client-side scanning proposals commonly discussed under the label “Chat Control.” This is a policy position, not legal advice: laws and proposals vary by jurisdiction and change over time. The project does not encourage unlawful content or conduct.

Its goal is practical self-hosting on modest infrastructure, including a free PythonAnywhere account, while documenting the security trade-offs honestly.
